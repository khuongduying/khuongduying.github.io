flowchart TD
    A[Patient with Sibling in Need of HSCT] --> B[Genetic Counseling]
    B --> C[HLA Typing of Family Members]
    C --> D[Design of HLA Markers/Probes]

    E --> F[Embryo Biopsy]
    F --> G1[PGT-M Analysis]

    G1 --> H[Embryo Selection]
